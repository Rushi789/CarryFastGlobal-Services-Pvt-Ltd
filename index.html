How Clearance Works

Four stages, start to finish.

The same sequence runs behind every consignment we handle — visible to you at each step.

01

Document Filing

Bill of entry / shipping bill, invoice and packing list are filed and matched against cargo details.

02

Assessment & Duty

HS classification is verified, duty is assessed, and payment is coordinated on your behalf.

03

Examination

Cargo is presented for customs examination where applicable, with our team on-site to resolve queries.

04

Clearance & Delivery

Out-of-charge is obtained and cargo moves to warehousing or directly to final-mile delivery.
